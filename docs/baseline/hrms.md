# Existing ERP baseline: HRMS (source: federated remote from erpforce-hrms-fe, mounted at /dashboard/hrms; the ERP repo itself has no HRMS source)

## Sidebar as-is (HRMS)
Dashboard | Organization Structure: Hierarchy, Department Master, Designation Master | Company Master Policy: Document Master, Company Calendar, Leave Policy Master, Salary Structure Master, Loan Configuration, Accruals and Benefit | Time Tracking: Time Card, Time Sheet | Employee Management: Employee Master, Employee Contracts | Asset Management: Asset List, Request, Transfer, Allocation, Tracker, Damage/Loss Claim, Asset Return | Approval Dashboard, Approval Work Flow | Recruitment: Hire Request, Talent Pool Dashboard, Candidate Management | Onboarding: Offer Letter | Leaves: Leave Management, Leave Request | Resource Management | Separation: Resignation, Termination | Payroll Management: Monthly Payroll, WPS Generation, Payslips | Loan Management: Loan Request | Reports (~35) | Settings: Forms, Template Editor. (Employee Assets, Employee Warning and Disciplinary, Employee Fines, Appraisal, Vacation List are commented out of the sidebar.)

## Employee Master
List: ID, Employee Name, Company, Department, Designation, Status (Draft/Active/Inactive). Tabs: Basic Details, Contract Details, Documents, Assets. Basic: names, email, phone, date of joining, dob, gender, nationality, company, department, designation, team, location, passport number, labour contract no, labour id, appraisal type, portal login, role. Contract: employment type, notice/probation period, working days/hours, salary structure, leave policy with allocations, accrual/benefit components, overtime rates. Documents table: document master, name, valid till date, remind before days, file, comments, status. Banks table (IBAN, SWIFT). Contracts screen (Draft, Approved, Active, Inactive).

## Leave
Leave Policy Master (tabs Leave Type, Eligibility Rules, Carry Forward, Encashment). Carry Forward EXISTS at policy level: enabled, max carry forward days, type Full or Limited, include balance display. Encashment configuration exists. Leave Management (employee): ID, Leave Type, Duration, From, To, No. of Days, Status, Document; statuses Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled. Leave Request (approver) with Approve/Reject and comment. Leave categories: Paid, Unpaid, Statutory, Annual, Other; accrual type Monthly, Yearly, Lump Sum.
NOT existing: Carry forward limit per Leave Type defaulting to 10 days with Excess Leave Treatment (Lapse / Encash / Capped Maximum Balance) and applicability per leave type as a rule, balance ledger, year-end rollover.

## Payroll, time, other
Time Card (Draft, Posted), Time Sheet (Draft, Submitted, Pending, Approved, Rejected), Monthly Payroll (Draft, Processed, Approved, Rejected), WPS Generation, Payslips, Salary Structure Master, Accruals and Benefit master (name, type, method, amount, frequency, base component, cap limit), Loans, Asset issuance (Asset List, Request, Allocation, Return), Recruitment, Resignation / Termination with final settlement and gratuity calculator, Approval Work Flow builder + Approval Dashboard, role-based HR Dashboard (employees by department, vacancies, leave requests, birthdays ...), ~35 reports (Employee List, Department Analysis, Employee Gratuity, Employee Salary, Payroll Variance, Time Sheet, Attendance Summary, Leave Request List, Annual Leave, Leave Encashment, Expiring Documents, Contract List, Final Settlement, Schedule Report ...).

## NOT existing
Ticket Allowance (Ticket Policy master, monthly accrual ledger, payout at interval, Ticket Accrual and Payout report; only report columns in a hidden Vacation List report). Document Compliance beyond Document Master fields and an Expiring Documents report: no reminder recipients per document type, no expiry notification log, no Document Expiry Dashboard. HR Warning Record entity (only a hidden Employee Warning report). Leave Management Dashboard (team calendar with carry forward status). Leave Balance Report as named. Notification log and audit trail screens.
